# Heating model: validation on held-out readings

171 real runs. Errors in C. Generated by `validate_heating.py`.

Chosen model (heating.py): waiting = the run's own straight line (else its batch's median slope); spinning = the Newton curve pooled over the run's batch. Sections 2 and 3 show why.

## 1. In-sample fit of the chosen model

| Phase | model | params from run | from batch | from all runs | median R^2 | median RMSE | parameters p10 / p50 / p90 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| wait | line | 135 | 36 | 0 | 0.986 | 0.53 | slope 0.198 / 0.230 / 0.306 C/s |
| spin | newton | 0 | 167 | 4 | 0.931 | 1.17 | T_inf 82 / 115 / 160 C, k 0.0026 / 0.0046 / 0.0098 1/s |
